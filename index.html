My research focuses on vulnerabilities that have real business impact, the kind that bypass security controls and expose sensitive data.

IDOR
Insecure Direct Object References: Accessing unauthorized resources through parameter manipulation to view, modify, or delete other users' data.

Privilege Escalation

🧩
Business Logic Flaws
